## Other decisions taken

1. **Tier math (A05/A06):** percentage steps only, legacy rounding rule. Fixed-amount tiers are not built; adding one later is additive.
2. **Gift quantity bound:** at most 5 per gift line, at most 5 gift lines per campaign (the proposal's numbers).
3. **A07 (selected-gift state):** gets its own small G3 review when reached; not decided here.
4. **Sequence of work** (each its own small PR, exact-head CI, merge): A02 → A03 → A04, B01 → B02 → B03, A06 → A07, B04, B05, B06. A08–A10 and C04 stay blocked on G2; B10/B11 on G5.
5. **Rollback posture:** every capability is default OFF; rollback is the flag. DB objects are never dropped once rows exist.

## Reversal

Any decision above can be reversed by the owner in one line; because nothing is enabled and no production data exists, reversal costs a follow-up PR, not a data migration.
